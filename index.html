About

I'm an IoT Development Engineer specializing in building end-to-end smart solutions that transform raw sensor data into actionable business intelligence

As an IoT Development Engineer, I design and deploy end-to-end IoT solutions for smart agriculture, environmental monitoring, and automation. My expertise spans sensor integration, cloud platforms (ThingSpeak, ThingsBoard), real-time MQTT data pipelines, and interactive dashboards using Power BI and Grafana.

I specialize in coding microcontrollers (ESP32, Raspberry Pi, Arduino), implementing IoT protocols (MQTT, HTTP, LoRa), and applying AI/Computer Vision (YOLOv8) to build intelligent, data-driven systems. Beyond engineering, I've trained 1500+ participants in IoT, Python, and data visualization.
